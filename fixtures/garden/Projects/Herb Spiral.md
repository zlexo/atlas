# Herb Spiral

A dry-stone spiral by the kitchen door, 1.8 m across and 80 cm high at the
centre. The top is lean and sharp-draining for rosemary and thyme; the foot
holds moisture for parsley and chives.

## Planting, top to bottom

1. Rosemary, sage
2. Thyme, oregano
3. [[Basil]] in summer, in pots sunk into the gap
4. Parsley, chives, mint (mint in a buried bucket)

Built over two weekends from reclaimed brick.
