# Raised Beds

Three beds along the south fence, each 1.2 m by 2.4 m and 30 cm deep. Untreated
larch boards, screwed into corner posts. Filled in layers: cardboard, branches,
half-rotted leaves, then a top third of sieved compost mixed with topsoil.

## Plan

- Bed 1: tomatoes and basil, see [[Companion Planting]].
- Bed 2: beans, then leeks after the beans come out.
- Bed 3: roots. Carrots and beetroot, thinned twice.

## Soil

Top up each spring with 5 cm of compost; the layers sink about that much a
year. See [[Compost Ratios]] for the heap that feeds them and [[Mulch]] for
what goes on top in summer.
