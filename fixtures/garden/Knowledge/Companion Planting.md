# Companion Planting

Pairs that have worked here, and one that has not.

| Pair | Why |
|---|---|
| Tomato and [[Basil]] | basil fills the gap under the trusses |
| Carrot and onion | the smells confuse carrot fly |
| Beans and sweetcorn | the corn is the bean pole |

Marigolds along the edge of the beds bring hoverflies, whose larvae eat
aphids. Fennel next to anything was a mistake: it sulks and so do its
neighbours.
