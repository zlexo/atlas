# Seed Saving

Save seed only from open-pollinated varieties; hybrids do not come true.
Tomatoes: scoop the seeds into water, leave three days to ferment, rinse and
dry on a plate. Beans: let the pods dry on the plant. Store in paper envelopes
in a tin, somewhere cool. The catalog of what is saved lives in the seed
catalog repo.
