# Tomato Blight

Late blight shows as brown patches on leaves and dark streaks on stems, and
spreads fast in warm, wet weeks. Once it is on a plant it does not go away.

## What helps

- Water the soil, never the leaves.
- Space plants for air; pinch out side shoots.
- Remove the lowest leaves once the first truss sets.
- Grow resistant varieties; keep a few under cover.

Burn or bin infected plants. They do not go on the heap, see [[Compost Ratios]].
